II

The blade

Contact, or it did not happen. Poise first. Then the body opens.

  1. Steel must touch. No look-assist. No phantom hits. Blood only where the edge lands.
  2. Two voices in the swing. Ours is a sharp slash. Theirs is a darker cut. Footsteps stay buried.
  3. Poise, then the window. Health and poise are separate. A break staggers. A parry or a break opens a short riposte.
  4. The bone is the rule. Head and neck take more. Hands and feet take less. Sever follows the skeleton, not the name of the enemy.
